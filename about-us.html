About YGJ

Growth is a journey,
not a moment.

Youth Growing Journey is a movement of young people becoming their best selves – built by someone who walked the same road and decided to share what changed his life.

Why YGJ exists

It started with a personal journey.

Most young men are not really living – they are just existing. Wake up, school, hours on the phone, sleep, repeat. YGJ was founded out of that exact experience: seeing friends lose themselves, and learning first-hand that discipline is the key to controlling everything in your life.

The goal is simple: pass on what actually helped. No secret formula – practical steps, honest words and a system that makes you show up every day.

“
If you are able to choose the hard, productive habits over the easy, cheap dopamine-inducing ones – that's when your life truly changes.
— A. Baumer, Founder of YGJ
From a young person,
for young people.

Mission

Help young people unlock their potential through discipline, high standards, growth, fitness, mindset, character and practical systems.

Vision

The biggest self-improvement app for young men, a real community with events – and a brand that stands for a strong generation.

Approach

Practical instead of just motivation. Remove what holds you back, build what moves you forward – step by step, with structure.

The mindset of control

Three beliefs we live by.

I am responsible.

I don't blame teachers, parents or society for everything. My actions shape my results. Even if life is unfair, I control my response.

I am capable.

I may not know everything today, but I can learn, adapt and grow. Skills can be developed, weaknesses turned into strengths.

I am persistent.

I won't stop because things get hard. Every obstacle is not a signal to quit – it's a test of my determination.
