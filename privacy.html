Privacy

Your data. Kept simple.

This website is an information page. It has no accounts, sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, your browser connects to GitHub's servers, which technically process your IP address to deliver the page.

Fonts & files

All fonts, images and icons are served from this website itself. No requests go to Google Fonts or other font services.

Instagram posts & reels

The homepage shows preview images of our Instagram posts that are stored on this website. Only when you tap a post is Instagram's official embed loaded from instagram.com. From that moment, Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. processes data (e.g. your IP address) according to its own privacy policy. Nothing is loaded from Instagram before you tap.

Contact form

If you send a message, your name, e-mail address, topic, subject and message are transmitted via the form service EmailJS (emailjs.com) – or, if it is unavailable, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) – to YGJ's inbox ygjcontact@gmail.com, solely to answer you. Data is only sent after you tick the consent box and press "Send". You can also write directly to ygjcontact@gmail.com.

Local storage

To prevent duplicate messages, the contact page stores the time of your last message in your own browser. The old YGJ website stored login data in your browser – this site deletes that data automatically when you visit.

External links

Links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and the Daily Tracker open those services. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Questions

For any privacy request, contact ygjcontact@gmail.com.
