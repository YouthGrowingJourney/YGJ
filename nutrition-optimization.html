The YGJ plate

Every meal, simplified.

½
Vegetables & fruit

Half your plate: colour, vitamins and fibre.

¼
Protein

Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, tofu or legumes.

¼
Smart carbs

Rice, potatoes, oats, whole-grain bread or pasta.

+
Water

Your default drink – with every meal, all day.

EnergySteady meals mean steady energy – no crash after lunch.
FocusYour brain runs on what you eat. Junk in, fog out.
MuscleTraining breaks muscle down, food builds it back up stronger.
SkinWhole foods and water show on your face.
MoodBlood sugar rollercoasters make you irritable and tired.
DisciplineChoosing what you eat is a daily discipline rep.
Protein every mealIt keeps you full and supports muscle growth.
Whole foods firstIf it grew, swam or walked, it's usually a good choice.
Fruit & vegetables dailyAim for colour on your plate at least twice a day.
Healthy fatsNuts, olive oil, avocado and fish are good for your brain.
Enough foodYour body is still growing. Eating too little is not discipline.
Sugary & energy drinksLiquid sugar and caffeine spikes – then the crash.
Ultra-processed snacksBuilt to make you eat more and feel worse.
Skipping mealsLeads to low energy and bingeing later.
Late heavy mealsA full stomach at bedtime hurts your sleep.
Eating while scrollingYou eat more and enjoy it less.
Plan aheadDecide what you eat before you're hungry.
Smart shopping listBuy the basics every week so good choices are always there.
80/20 ruleEat well 80% of the time. The other 20% won't ruin you.
Healthy snacks readyFruit, nuts, yogurt – make the good choice the easy choice.
Learn 3 simple mealsBeing able to cook basics is a real-life superpower.
Water first thingA glass of water after waking up wakes your body up.
Drink regularlyAround 2 litres a day – more when you train or it's hot.
Carry a bottleIf it's with you, you'll drink it.
Limit caffeineEspecially in the afternoon – it steals your sleep.
Check your colourLight yellow means you're hydrated. Dark means drink more.
