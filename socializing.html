Socializing & Confidence.

Confidence isn't loud. Learn to connect, hold a conversation and build a circle that pushes you forward.

5 chapters26 rules6 min read
Every conversation

Four steps to connect.

01
Eye contact

Look people in the eye when you greet and when you listen.

02
Ask

Open questions: "How did you get into that?" instead of yes/no.

03
Listen

Really listen – then build your next question on their answer.

04
Follow up

Remember details and bring them up next time.

ConfidenceEvery good conversation proves to you that you can do it.
OpportunitiesJobs, teams, friendships – most doors open through people.
HappinessStrong friendships are one of the biggest drivers of a good life.
NetworkThe people around you shape who you become.
ResilienceHard times are easier with people who have your back.
LeadershipLeaders are people others want to follow – that starts with connection.
Open body languageStand tall, shoulders relaxed, phone in your pocket.
Eye contactNot staring – just steady and calm.
Remember namesUse someone's name once in the conversation. People notice.
Open questionsAsk about what people like, do or think – not just facts.
Listen more than you talkBeing interested makes you interesting.
Phone as a shieldHiding in your phone tells everyone you don't want to talk.
Seeking validationTrying to impress everyone makes you seem needy, not confident.
OverthinkingNobody remembers your awkward moment as much as you do.
ComparisonSomeone else's highlight reel isn't your real life.
Negative self-talk"They won't like me" is a guess, not a fact.
Join somethingA team, club, gym or project – shared goals create real friendships.
Be the one who invitesDon't wait to be asked. Most people are waiting too.
Choose people who push youYou become the average of the people you spend time with.
Quality over quantityThree real friends beat a hundred followers.
Be reliableShow up when you say you will. Trust is built in small moments.
One new conversationTalk to one person you don't usually talk to – every day.
Genuine complimentsNotice something real and say it.
Speak upAnswer a question in class or share your opinion once a day.
Phone away with friendsBe fully there when you're with people.
ReflectWhat went well today? What will you try tomorrow?
